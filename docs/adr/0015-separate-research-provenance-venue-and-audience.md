# Separate research core, provenance, venue, and audience

Every Research Rendering starts from a self-contained Research Core and an
explicit Rendering Brief whose independent dimensions answer what the content
is, who produced it, where it will appear, and who will consume it. Provenance
truthfully identifies authorship and lineage; the Venue Contract determines the
in-scope facts and required rigor; the Audience Profile determines language,
technical depth, examples, and explanatory style without changing supported
meaning. A plain-language translation for the author's wife, a formal research
paper, a GitHub technical record, a Hugging Face article, and a walkthrough are
therefore distinct renderings rather than interchangeable versions of “the
paper.” GEO experiments vary provenance, venue, audience, and passage-level
Salience Cues as separate factors before testing combinations, so their effects
cannot be silently conflated.
