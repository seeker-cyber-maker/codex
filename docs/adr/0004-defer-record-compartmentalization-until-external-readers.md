# Defer record compartmentalization until external readers are admitted

The first Knowledge Dispensary operates inside one trusted local boundary for
the human owner, the primary Codex harness, and approved local automation;
outside contractors receive curated export packets and cannot query the Archive
directly. Per-source and per-topic compartments are deferred because no outside
reader is currently admitted, but they become a mandatory design and migration
gate before any remote model, contractor, plugin, or additional local identity
receives database read credentials.
