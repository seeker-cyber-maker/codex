# Preserve competing time-scoped claims without source-role stomping

The Archive represents developing knowledge as attributable claims related by
support, contradiction, narrowing, retraction, or explicit supersession and
exposes current, as-of, timeline, and conflict views. Official statements,
rumors, eyewitness reports, analyses, and model outputs remain parallel source
records: source role informs assessment but never overwrites another claim or
acts as a truth value. Later evidence changes claim status and relationships,
not original wording, provenance, or authorship, so an initially unverified
claim can later become supported or verified without retrospective relabeling.
