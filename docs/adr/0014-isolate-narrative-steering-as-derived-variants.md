# Isolate narrative-steering experiments as derived variants

A summary-fuzzer or GEO contractor operates on an immutable manuscript revision
and emits a signed Narrative Variant with an attributable transformation rather
than editing canonical prose in place. Synthetic narrative, exploratory
wording, and intentionally adversarial insertions remain reversible derived
material; incorporating any of them into the manuscript requires a separate
deliberate editorial contribution and attestation. This preserves a reproducible
record of steering experiments without allowing optimization artifacts or
nonsense inserted to game another model to silently degrade the paper.
