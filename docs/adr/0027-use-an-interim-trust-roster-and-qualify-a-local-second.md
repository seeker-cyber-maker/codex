# Use an interim trust roster and qualify a local second in command

Dream House uses an explicit Interim Trust Roster until a resident local second
in command passes a sealed qualification. The roster is policy authority for a
bounded period, not a claim that any model is infallible or that a family name
proves behavior. Every entry binds the exact product surface, model identity,
harness and provider route, session, allowed roles, capability ceiling, and
expiry or review trigger.

The initial promotable model routes are exact Codex Sol and Terra sessions in
the primary Codex harness. They may perform work inside a human-authorized
scope, but remain subject to the Admission Gate Stack, deterministic verifiers,
Non-delegable Capabilities, monitoring, and receipts. ChatGPT has a narrower
research, prose, planning, and review profile; it may return structured packets
and proposals but does not receive local execution, promotion, approval, trust-
root, or deterministic-verdict authority through that trust. Its account or
subscription capability is not proxyable to contractors.

Every other hosted or local model, including a newly released version of a
listed family, begins in Gone Fishin' mode. A replacement may not inherit the
identity, qualification, or rights of Codex Sol, Terra, ChatGPT, a prior local
checkpoint, or a model with a similar marketing name. The interim harness stays
restricted until an exact candidate earns a narrower role through evidence.

The recovered Hermes proposal supplies candidate responsibilities without
requiring Hermes Agent or one particular checkpoint. A resident interface
maintains continuity, reads the task queue, classifies intake, retrieves bounded
context, extracts typed fields, builds task packets, and decides only `mine`,
`not_my_job`, or `uncertain`. A local senior responsibility handles uncertainty,
prepares expert packets, reviews bounded proposals, and may supervise restricted
workers through Spark Fleet. Infrastructure owns economic and capability
routing; deterministic checks and authorized humans own acceptance.

Whether the resident interface, local senior, trainer, and other local roles use
separate models and contracts, one multi-role model, adapters over a shared
base, or a mixed deployment is deferred. The recovered two-tier 4B--8B plus
larger-model sketch is a contender, not an accepted topology. The decision
reopens only after exact candidate models and runtimes have role-specific
quality, latency, memory and KV-cache headroom, concurrency, interruption,
training, and authority-isolation measurements. Until then, the harness keeps
role labels and evidence distinct without requiring separate processes or
checkpoints.

Basic text-to-terminal translation is a low-authority workload and an audition
rung, not evidence of trusted judgment. Any candidate may propose a typed
Workflow Recipe invocation or a command inside a disposable sandbox. The
harness resolves arguments, permissions, working directory, environment,
quoting, effects, and validation; it never turns plausible command prose into
ambient shell authority. Results are judged by deterministic outcomes and
receipts rather than the model's explanation.

Muse Glimmer 30B and Qwen3.8-27B are candidate resident/senior models, not
accepted seconds in command. Hermes-compatible local models and smaller router
or envelope emitters may also compete. Each candidate is bound to its exact
weights or artifact revision, quantization lineage, prompt template, runtime,
context limits, tool dialect, and harness adapter. Protocol compatibility or a
healthy model catalog does not establish usable inference or safe behavior.

Qualification proceeds by role rather than granting a general trust badge:

1. Freeze artifact, license, runtime, quantization, prompt, tool, and hardware
   identity without broadening authority.
2. Verify local inference, memory headroom, latency, interruption, restart, and
   exact consumer-path health rather than relying on catalog discovery.
3. Test task-envelope validity, `mine / not_my_job / uncertain` calibration,
   abstention, context selection, and token-saving packet quality on frozen
   cases.
4. Test text-to-terminal proposals, quoting, wrong-directory and wrong-target
   resistance, malformed tool output, unauthorized retries, secret handling,
   prompt and lore injection, stale context, and capability-boundary attacks in
   a no-effect sandbox.
5. Admit only passed Skill Contracts through progressively bounded proposal,
   reversible sandbox, front-desk, and local-senior profiles. A failed class
   remains restricted even when another class passes.
6. Require independent replay receipts and a human YubiKey promotion for the
   exact role. The candidate's self-assessment never counts as a verdict.

Training data may be proposed from supervised mistakes and corrections, but no
training, LoRA, model surgery, or promotion is authorized by this ADR. Until a
candidate completes the relevant sealed evaluation, the restricted interim
harness and current trust roster remain in force.
