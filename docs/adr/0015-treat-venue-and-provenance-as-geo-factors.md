# Treat venue and provenance as first-class GEO factors

Venue, provenance, audience, and format can change a model's expectations and
salience judgments even when the underlying claims are unchanged, so every GEO
run records and controls its Venue Frame rather than treating it as neutral
metadata. A research paper, Hugging Face article, and technical walkthrough are
distinct Publication Artifacts derived from a common evidence and claim record;
they may differ in structure, pacing, explanation, and preview strategy without
becoming interchangeable copies or changing the supported findings. Venue-frame
conditions are tested separately from passage-level Salience Cues before any
factorial combination, preventing a paper-versus-article prior from being
misreported as a marker or wording effect.
