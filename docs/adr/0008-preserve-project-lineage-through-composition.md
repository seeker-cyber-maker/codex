# Preserve project lineage through composition and capability references

Cross-project relationships live in an overlay and do not rewrite either
project's native taxonomy or history. A combined effort such as QP2A plus
TIS-100 is a separately governed composite that references the standalone
source projects; a general-purpose kernel remains an independent capability
provider consulted by QP2A, SNO, or other consumers rather than being merged
into their main branches. This follows the Dream House upstream-first pattern:
reuse exact interfaces and evidence while keeping source, composite, and shared
factory authority distinct.
