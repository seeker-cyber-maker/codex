# After-action review: TERM compatibility preflight v1

## Outcome

The source-only gate is now mechanically distinguishable from a real model
experiment. The critical negative property—no execution authority—has a direct
test instead of existing only in prose.

## Gaps retained

The corpus is synthetic and evaluator-visible only. It cannot prove that TERM
notation reduces false agreement, survives compaction, works across lineages,
or deserves dialect escalation. Those questions remain open until a separately
authorized run binds roster, decoding, isolation, and scoring evidence.

## Process note

The initial test failure on a placeholder semantic hash was useful: it showed
that the validator enforced corpus identity before the preflight could pass.
