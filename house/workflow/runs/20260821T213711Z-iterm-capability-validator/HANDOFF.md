## Acceptance boundary

This accepts only the pure validator. The future live adapter must supply a
trusted monotonic clock, collapse internal rejection codes to one external
response, prevent bearer logging/history persistence, define process restart
and refresh behavior, prove WKWebView request-header behavior, and undergo a
new review before it binds a socket or registers with iTerm.
