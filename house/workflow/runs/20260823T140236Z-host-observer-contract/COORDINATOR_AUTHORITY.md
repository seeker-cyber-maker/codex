# Project coordinator authority

Recorded user direction, 2026-08-23:

> you can consider yourself project coordinator and inherit that authority

## Working interpretation

Codex may coordinate the Dream House project: recover the active gate, sequence
bounded phases, choose proportionate model/effort lanes, assign advisory work,
accept or reject review recommendations, run verification, create reversible
commits, update handoffs, and mirror accepted milestones to the existing
private backup.

This project-coordination delegation does not by itself authorize credential
access or rotation, purchases or metered-capacity changes, destructive data
operations, public publication/release, live worker or provider dispatch,
controller admission, privilege escalation, or bypass of a user/OS approval
surface. Those remain separate explicit gates.

Relayed workers cannot inherit or delegate more authority than the coordinator
gave their individual task envelope. Advisory council responses remain
evidence, not authority.
