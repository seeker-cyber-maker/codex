# Real local runner council — plan v1

## Objective

Obtain a blind, local-only outside-council design disposition for the smallest
safe implementation slice that could move Dream House from mock-only worker
contracts toward one real, operator-authorized `codex exec` observation.

## Non-goals

- Do not launch Codex, contact a provider, acquire a controller lease, create a
  live intent, touch a YubiKey, consume authority, or mutate the prepared MCU
  operation.
- Do not implement source changes during the council run.
- Do not treat council advice as human authority or execution approval.

## Council

Three blind reviewers receive the same immutable packet: evidence auditor,
constructive theorist, and adversarial methodologist. The run is local-only,
same-model/shared-host, with no external provider calls and no reviewer access
to other round-one responses.

## Acceptance

The run closes when all attempted reviewers have a recorded disposition and
packet-hash status, and the chair records an evidence-weighted decision of
`ACCEPT_DESIGN`, `REVISE_DESIGN`, or `BLOCKED`, plus one smallest next action.
