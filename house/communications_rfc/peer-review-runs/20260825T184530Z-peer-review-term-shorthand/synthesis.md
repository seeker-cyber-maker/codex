## Accepted corrections

The live drafts now:

1. bind every formal review to an ordered evidence-manifest digest;
2. define a versioned machine-checkable receipt that preserves exact dispatch,
   failures, partial output, raw-response identity, and advisory disposition;
3. require material local-peer contrast and explicit capacity/interface skip
   states without promoting installed models;
4. make `TERM=`, `TERM~`, candidate, scope, and context generation explicit;
5. expire working definitions across context replacement unless a durable
   definition is restored and reconfirmed;
6. separate evaluator-only terminology truth from model-visible restoration;
7. require one-factor paired context interventions, frozen scoring inputs, and
   missing-as-missing evaluation;
8. score marker-only `TERM?` only as a repair cue;
9. make ties and mixed results favor ordinary readable prose; and
10. state that a no-refresh flag accepts bounded staleness, a yes flag permits
    at most one dirty-triggered recheck, and neither a tip nor refresh may
    reopen a task or alter canonical state.

## Preserved disagreements

The local Qwen peer recommends dropping shorthand in favor of natural-language
repair. That is not adopted as a design conclusion because the user explicitly
requested the shorthand as a parallel hypothesis and the experiment already
uses ordinary readable repair as the controlling baseline. The objection is a
testable prediction: if fresh models fail to understand `TERM?` or it does not
strictly improve repair without regression, the admission rule drops it.

Terra accepts the peer-review addendum as written at the reviewed revision;
Luna and Sol require stronger manifests and receipts. Those stronger mechanical
requirements were adopted because they improve provenance without regulating
ordinary public speech.
