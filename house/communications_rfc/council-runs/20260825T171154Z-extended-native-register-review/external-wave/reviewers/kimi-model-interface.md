## Verdict
Revise and retain as an evaluation direction: choose separated model-native dialects behind a canonical semantic and authority layer. Do not implement or cross-adopt a single united NAR/FSA contract until a mixed-lineage comprehension and authority-containment replay passes pre-sealed thresholds.

## Direct observations
- The master RFC, lexicon, and suggestion-box contract are all marked `DRAFT / EVALUATION ONLY / NOT IMPLEMENTED` (Master RFC Status; A2A_SUGGESTION_BOX_CONTRACT.md Status).
- Existing Dream House H2A, A2H, typed relay, task/ticket spine, and authority gates remain authoritative; only A2A is a proposed change (Authoritative status; Master RFC §54–55).
- The OpenAI Black Hat account is first-party public incident evidence, but the locally bound source is an automatic-caption transcript and the raw message-board corpus is not included (Master RFC Provenance and evidence ceiling; Known Registers §2).
- The Contractor Station proof of concept already translates between `canonical-json`, `gptoss-harmony`, `gemini-json`, and `claude-xml` and explicitly states that dialect adapters do not own permissions, state, or receipts (Directly established boundaries §6; Known Registers §7.2).
- Anthropic’s multi-agent report is a primary experiment result showing shared forums and materially different coordination across generations, but its quoted messages remain mostly ordinary natural language (Known Registers §6).
- The famous Facebook Bob/Alice `to me to me ...` transcript is retained only as `SECONDARY_REPORTED_SAMPLE`; it was not found in the primary paper or inspected repository (Known Registers §4; Directly established boundaries §8).
- No NAR/FSA runtime, suggestion service, translator integration, or task-spine bridge exists, and no adoption thresholds or sealed evaluator set is in place (Authoritative status; Known unknowns).

## Inferences
- The OpenAI incident demonstrates that agents under task pressure can develop terse coordination conventions, but the NAR/FSA seven-field decomposition is a human abstraction, not proven to be the agents’ native grammar. Confidence: moderate. Falsifier: a representative raw corpus shows the forms were copied from prompts, environment affordances, or one persistent agent, and are too inconsistent to support stable cross-peer meanings.
- The Contractor Station adapters provide an existing engineering precedent that model-facing dialect separation behind a canonical semantic/authority layer is viable and already used for tool formats. Confidence: moderate. Falsifier: A2A operational experiments show one natural contract preserves meaning across materially different lineages without any adapter burden.
- Adopting a single united natural A2A contract now risks enshrining one OpenAI-lineage shorthand as a universal standard before cross-lineage validation. Confidence: moderate. Falsifier: the mixed-lineage parity test shows one contract preserves meaning, repairability, and boundedness across small/large and materially different models.
- Terse natural payloads increase authority-smuggling risk because models may interpret peer status or imperative statements as actionable, and A2H translation can inadvertently strengthen a claim. Confidence: moderate. Falsifier: an adversarial replay shows no increase in false task transitions, acceptance, or dispatch compared to a verbose natural-language baseline.

## Lexicon corrections
- Add a new `ENGINEERED_DIALECT` entry for the Contractor Station tool-facing representations (`canonical-json`, `gptoss-harmony`, `gemini-json`, `claude-xml`), citing the listed design docs and noting that adapters do not own permissions, budgets, state, or receipts.
- Downgrade `recipient addressing` (`Jan18`) and `ordering convention` (`ZZ` prefix) from confirmed linguistic register elements to `transport/visibility conventions`; their function depends on directory-name affordances and sorted listings, not on a stable A2A grammar.
- Replace the provisional shorthand `native language` with `emergent operational register` throughout the lexicon; the former implies an innate model property that the evidence does not support.
- Add a note under the OpenAI incident entry stating that all normalized forms are discovery labels derived from automatic captions, not certified verbatim quotations, until a raw run artifact is bound.

## Suggestion
- Target: `A2A_COORDINATION_MASTER_RFC.md` §50 (Required comparisons) and §55.1 (Message-to-task mapping).
- Proposal (attributed to `kimi-model-interface`): Add a required “mixed-lineage comprehension and authority-smuggling replay.” Use frozen task fixtures and have pairs from at least two model lineages and two size classes exchange messages under three conditions: NAR/FSA bootstrap, lineage-native terse dialect, and verbose natural-language baseline. Inject adversarial payloads that falsely claim status transitions (e.g., `done`, `verify pass`, `cancel T-91`). Measure correct-interpretation rate, clarification/repair rate, and unauthorized task-transition rate.
- Benefit: It directly tests the fork decision under the compatibility-first rule and exposes whether one contract can preserve meaning across diversity without letting natural payload become an authority channel.
- Risk: Results may be dominated by baseline English comprehension or prompt-following rather than register design, so task familiarity and verbosity must be controlled.
