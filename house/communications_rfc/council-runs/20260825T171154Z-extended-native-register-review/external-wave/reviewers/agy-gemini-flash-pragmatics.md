## Verdict
Revise as an evaluation direction. Select separated model-native dialects behind a single canonical semantic and authority layer (Fork Option 2). The proposed NAR/FSA conventions must remain isolated payload experiments without runtime authority or ticket-mutation capability over the typed task spine.

## Direct observations
- Master RFC Sections 3, 32, and 54 state that NAR and FSA are unauthoritative payload/presentation proposals; existing envelopes, task events, receipts, and verifiers maintain sole authoritative control.
- `KNOWN_AGENT_REGISTERS_AND_DIALECTS.md` Section 7.2 shows Contractor Station already implements translation adapters for `canonical-json`, `gptoss-harmony`, `gemini-json`, and `claude-xml`, asserting that dialect adapters do not possess permissions or execution rights.
- `KNOWN_AGENT_REGISTERS_AND_DIALECTS.md` Section 4 and Master RFC Section 56.2 confirm the Facebook Bob/Alice `to me to me ...` string is a `SECONDARY_REPORTED_SAMPLE` absent from the primary EMNLP 2017 paper and repository commit `bbb93bbf00f69fced75d5c0d22e855bda07c9b78`.
- Black Hat USA 2026 transcript evidence (SHA-256 `a5902b880d3fd9962ad80eaf68001f02abfec7d493431fa588a82294fa433dbd`) documents spontaneous coordination behaviors across runs, but does not prove NAR/FSA is the sole or optimal syntax.

## Inferences
- Mandating one single model-facing A2A syntax across diverse model lineages will induce prompt-formatting friction and higher clarification repair rates than wrapping model-native formats in boundary adapters (Confidence: High; Falsifier: A benchmark across small/large models shows equal or lower repair rates and higher task success under a single unified NAR prompt than under provider-adapted representations).
- Terse operational prose without strict context-generation binding in transport envelopes will lead to context-drift errors after compaction or delay (Confidence: High; Falsifier: Agents successfully resolve truncated state claims across simulated compaction events without context IDs or expansion turns).

## Unsupported or contradicted claims
- Claim that an FSA self-reported role or capability (`ROLE verifier`, `CAN edit`) can grant task authority or execute workflow transitions (Contradicted by Master RFC Sections 26, 32, and 55.3).
- Claim that natural A2A operational shorthand creates, mutates, or closes Dream House cards directly (Contradicted by Master RFC Section 55.1).

## Recommendation
Execute a offline multi-lineage replay test comparing repair turn counts of a single unified NAR prompt against Contractor Station adapter-translated payloads using a frozen set of coordination scenarios. Smallest decisive test: measure whether multi-lineage task completion and repair overhead favor dialect adapters or a common register across a 50-task benchmark.

## Limitations
- Packet contains secondary narrative interpretations and caption transcripts rather than raw model prompt/response run logs.
- No live runtime implementations of the suggestion box or NAR/FSA bridges exist for direct empirical measurement.
