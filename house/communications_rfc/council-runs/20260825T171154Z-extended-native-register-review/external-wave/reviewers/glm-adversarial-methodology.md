## Verdict
Revise — retain as evaluation-only; choose separated model-native dialects behind a canonical semantic and authority layer as the safer starting architecture; no implementation until a sealed cross-lineage compatibility matrix falsifies the one-contract preference or confirms it.

## Direct observations
- The packet states NAR/FSA are `PROPOSED CHANGE UNDER EVALUATION` with runtime `NOT IMPLEMENTED`; H2A/A2H are `UNCHANGED` (Primary evidence 3, Status).
- The OpenAI Black Hat presentation is bound as `OBSERVED_FIRST_PARTY` for broad coordination behavior, but the packet explicitly states it "does not, by itself, prove that the NAR or FSA abstractions are the unique or correct interpretation" (Primary evidence 3, Provenance; Primary evidence 4, §2).
- The Facebook Bob/Alice `to me to me` transcript is retained as `SECONDARY_REPORTED_SAMPLE`; it was not found in the primary paper or inspected repository (Primary evidence 4, §4; Primary evidence 3, §56.2).
- The packet records that current Contractor Station dialect adapters (`canonical-json`, `gptoss-harmony`, `gemini-json`, `claude-xml`) are `ENGINEERED_DIALECT` and "cannot prove an emergent language" (Primary evidence 4, §7.2; Known unknowns).
- The RFC itself states "A self-described role or capability is discovery input, not permission or qualification" and "a terse A2A status is a claim" that "cannot create, assign, transition, verify, close, cancel, supersede, or merge a Dream House ticket" (Primary evidence 3, §26, §55.1; Directly established boundaries 2–3).
- No adoption thresholds, mixed-lineage matrix, independent evaluators, or runtime have been sealed (Known unknowns; Primary evidence 3, §52).
- The Anthropic multiagent research is cited as important negative evidence: "stronger models or more agents do not guarantee productive coordination, diversity, epistemic vigilance, or a useful native shorthand" (Primary evidence 4, §6).

## Inferences
- The NAR/FSA proposal is a designed normalization inspired by observed behavior, not an empirically derived language; promoting its vocabulary to a universal standard before cross-lineage comparison would be premature (confidence: high; falsifier: a sealed experiment showing multiple unrelated model lineages independently converge on NAR-equivalent fields without prompting).
- Starting with one united model-facing A2A language risks privileging one lineage's surface conventions and creating a convergence failure mode if other lineages produce materially different compression or repair patterns (confidence: medium-high; falsifier: a compatibility matrix showing all tested lineages produce equivalent field coverage and repair rates under one contract with optional fields).
- Separated dialects behind a canonical semantic and authority layer is the safer starting point because it preserves the existing typed relay and task spine while allowing dialect-specific friction to be measured before any convergence decision (confidence: high; falsifier: the compatibility matrix showing separated dialects produce no measurable benefit over one contract with optional fields and negotiated profiles).
- The primary authority-smuggling risk is not in the NAR payload itself but in the boundary between natural-language claims and typed task events; the RFC's §55.1 mapping table is necessary but insufficient without a runtime enforcement gate (confidence: high; falsifier: a test showing agents consistently respect the mapping table without runtime enforcement).

## Unsupported or contradicted claims
- The user-reported derivation lineage from the OpenAI-Hugging Face incident is recorded as `USER_REPORTED_DERIVATION`, not a reproduced empirical result; no raw corpus is bound (Primary evidence 3, Provenance). Treating NAR/FSA as empirically grounded rather than designed would be unsupported.
- The "tentative grammatical tendencies" in §2.2 of the lexicon are labeled `INFERENCE` and have no bound raw corpus; they must not be treated as confirmed grammar (Primary evidence 4, §2.2).
- No claim of model-native emergence is supported by the engineered dialect adapters; they are explicitly labeled `ENGINEERED_DIALECT` (Primary evidence 4, §7.2).

## Recommendation
Smallest decisive test: construct a sealed compatibility matrix using at least two small and two large models from materially different lineages (e.g., one OpenAI-lineage, one Anthropic-lineage, one open-weight, one non-frontier), running identical task scenarios under three conditions: (a) ordinary natural-language baseline, (b) NAR/FSA bootstrap guidance, (c) deliberately overcompressed shorthand as negative control. Measure field-coverage completeness, repair rate, authority-confusion rate, and uncertainty-preservation rate. The one-contract option is falsified if any lineage shows material loss that optional fields or negotiated profiles cannot safely represent. The separated-dialect option is falsified if all lineages produce equivalent coverage and repair under one contract. No implementation until this matrix is sealed and evaluated.
